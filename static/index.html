Toy, not science. A face model (FairFace, ResNet-34 trained on a balanced 108k-face dataset) reads seven broad groups from pixels; the finer population split comes from a vision model when one is configured, otherwise from the shape of that distribution. Nothing here reads DNA: it is a phenotype resemblance guess, not ancestry or nationality. Photos are processed locally and deleted immediately.
